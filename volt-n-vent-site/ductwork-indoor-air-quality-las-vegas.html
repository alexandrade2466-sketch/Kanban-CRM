Ductwork Replacement & Airflow Correction In Las Vegas

Many Las Vegas homes were built with duct systems that are undersized, poorly sealed, poorly balanced, or simply aged out. When ductwork is restricted, your system has to fight harder to move air.

Undersized Ductwork

When ducts are too small, static pressure increases and newer ECM blower motors can be forced to work harder than designed.

  • Weak airflow
  • Noisy registers
  • Hot and cold rooms
  • Reduced equipment life

Dust, Dirt & Contamination

Old duct systems can collect dust, pet dander, construction debris, fibers, allergens, and other particles.

  • Dirty supply vents
  • Dust returning quickly
  • Odors from old duct systems
  • Poorer indoor air quality

Air Balance Problems

True comfort comes from properly sized, sealed, distributed, and balanced airflow throughout the entire home.

  • Uneven temperatures
  • Longer run times
  • Higher utility bills
  • Harder compressor operation

Why Ductwork Matters More With Modern High-Efficiency Equipment

ECM motors, inverter systems, high-SEER equipment, and variable-speed technology all rely on proper duct design.

Old Ductwork Can Choke New Equipment

Replacing the AC system without addressing ductwork may leave the new equipment operating under the same airflow problems.

Balanced Airflow Can Extend Equipment Life

A properly designed and balanced duct system can reduce strain, improve comfort, lower operating costs, and help extend equipment life.

Three-Part Indoor Air Quality Protection

A strong IAQ strategy should address particles, odors, biological growth, airborne contaminants, and surface cleanliness inside the HVAC system.

1. Particle Filtration

Upgrade from basic filters to higher-performance filtration for dust, pollen, lint, dander, and airborne particles.

  • MERV 13 filtration
  • Aprilaire media filter cabinets
  • Large surface-area filters
  • Reduced dust circulation

2. Odor & Chemical Reduction

Carbon filtration can help reduce certain odors and airborne chemical smells that standard filters may not address.

  • Carbon filter options
  • Pet and household odor support
  • Smoke and cooking odor reduction
  • Improved indoor freshness

3. Surface & Coil Cleaning

UV lights and bipolar ionization can help support a cleaner HVAC environment and improve your air purification strategy.

  • UV filtration options
  • Bipolar ionization
  • Evaporator coil surface support
  • Cleaner system operation

Ductwork & IAQ FAQs

How much does ductwork replacement cost in Las Vegas?

Duct system change-outs start at $8,500 for qualifying like-for-like replacements. Final pricing depends on home size, duct routing, sealing needs, and accessibility.

How much do indoor air quality packages cost?

IAQ packages start as low as $1,500 depending on MERV 13 or Aprilaire filtration, carbon filters, UV, ionization, and electrical requirements.

Should I replace ductwork when I replace my AC?

If your ducts are undersized, leaky, or contaminated, new equipment may not perform as expected until airflow is corrected. We review both during consultations.

What is included in a three-part IAQ strategy?

Particle filtration (MERV 13 / Aprilaire), odor and chemical reduction (carbon), and surface or coil support (UV / bipolar ionization) based on your goals.

Do you install zoning systems?

Yes. Zoning requires proper dampers, bypass planning, controls, and duct design — we evaluate compatibility with your existing equipment.
