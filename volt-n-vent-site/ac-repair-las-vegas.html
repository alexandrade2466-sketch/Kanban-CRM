Las Vegas AC Repair FAQs

What are the most common AC repairs in Las Vegas?

Capacitors, contactors, condenser fan motors, blower motors, clogged drain lines, thermostat issues, and refrigerant-related problems are among the most common AC repairs in the Las Vegas Valley.

Can you recharge my AC with refrigerant?

Yes, when appropriate. However, low refrigerant usually means there is a leak. We can check the system, verify refrigerant levels, and explain repair options before adding refrigerant.

Why is my AC running but not cooling?

Common causes include a failed capacitor, dirty condenser coil, low refrigerant, blower problem, frozen evaporator coil, failed compressor, thermostat issue, or restricted airflow.

Do you service rental properties?

Do you also handle electrical issues related to AC equipment?
